What to expect

You get a scenario, reference images and instructions at the start. Generate an original AI video and submit it as an MP4 before time runs out.

You're judged on prompt engineering, scenario accuracy, visual quality and creativity.

Rules

  • Bring your own laptop, tool accounts and credits.
  • Runway, Kling, Pika, Sora, CapCut, Flow and similar tools are allowed.
  • AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude may help with prompts.
  • Videos must be created during the 120 minutes. Pre-made videos are not accepted.
  • Don't copy another team's prompts or fabricate generation evidence.
  • Judges may inspect your prompts. Their decision is final.
